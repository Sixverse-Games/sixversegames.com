01 / 09 ABOUT THE GAME

Forge your own playstyle

Wild World Online is a multiplayer third-person werewolf action game. There is no single right way to play. Choose your character, unlock your abilities and show your rivals the power of your style.

02 / 09

Stand in front, take the hits

Raise your guard, absorb the blows, heal yourself and get back in. Carry the weight of the fight while your team strikes from behind you.

03 / 09

Shape the fight with spells

Where claws aren’t enough, your spells do the talking. Strike from range with Magic Bolt and pull rivals in with Gravity. Fire, Frost, Electric or Dark: pick your element and turn the fight your way.

04 / 09

Vanish, then strike

Disappear into the shadows, watch your target and wait for the moment. Then come out behind them, close the gap with a dash and cut them down.

05 / 09

Move like a wolf

Run along walls, drop from above and use the map as your own path. Movement is a weapon too.

06 / 09

Every hit lands somewhere

With locational damage, where you strike matters. A well-placed hit deals more damage and can swing the fight.

07 / 09

Hunt with your pack

Survive on your own in free-for-all, or hunt with your pack in team matches. And when the round is yours, howl together.

08 / 09

Make the screen yours

Choose segmented or classic health bars for yourself and for other players, and tune combat HUD options like hit shake to your taste.

09 / 09

Coming soon

We keep building the game with feedback from our closed tests. Next up: a respawn button, a surrender vote, new balance changes, new cosmetics, new abilities and new emotes.
