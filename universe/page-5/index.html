The God Emperor Rises

The God Emperor Rises I

He felt a thirst rising within.

Through countless wars he fed on the blood and the souls of Ezelîler. With every war their numbers dwindled, while he went on learning, and went on consuming.

From Primal Magic to Blood Magic, from Shadow Magic to Elemental Magic, he learned them all. Now he could not be wounded, and he could not be killed. There was no fleeing from him, and no hiding from him.

Those Ezelîler who once boasted of his victories now strove for his death, and gave him impossible tasks. But it was too late. Their pride and their fear had already prepared their end.
