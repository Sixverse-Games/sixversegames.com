Developer Diary #1: the October 18 closed Steam test

In Wild World Online there is no single right way to play: you forge your own playstyle. Be the tank who guards and soaks up the damage, shape the fight with Magic Bolt and Gravity, or vanish and strike from the shadows.

On October 18 we ran a closed Steam test. In this developer diary: footage from the test and what comes next.

What we tested

  • Wall running and parkour
  • Spells
  • Health bar styles (segmented and classic)
  • Locational damage
  • Free-for-all and team matches

Coming soon

  • Respawn button
  • Surrender vote
  • New balance changes
  • New cosmetics, abilities and emotes
